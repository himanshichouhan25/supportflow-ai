# ACCOUNT POLICY

## 1. Account Information & Verification
- Customer accounts are uniquely identified by a customer ID (e.g. C101).
- Customer details include registered name, email address, phone number, and primary shipping address.

## 2. Ownership & Security Rules
- Support operations modifying orders, returns, or refunds require verification that the requesting customer owns the account and order.
- Security-sensitive actions (email update, phone number change, password reset) require strict customer verification.

## 3. Escalation Conditions
- Suspected account compromise, unauthorized order placement, or email/phone mismatch issues trigger support ticket escalation.
