# DELIVERY POLICY

## 1. Delivery Status Meanings
- PENDING / CONFIRMED: Order is being prepared for shipment at the warehouse.
- SHIPPED / IN_TRANSIT: Order has been dispatched with a logistics carrier.
- DELIVERED: Order has been successfully handed over to the customer address.
- DELAYED: Order shipment is experiencing unexpected carrier delay.

## 2. Tracking & Status Inquiries
- Customers can query tracking information, estimated delivery dates, and carrier details using their valid order ID.
- Tracking status is updated deterministically from carrier partner feeds.

## 3. Delayed Delivery Handling
- Deliveries marked DELAYED for more than 5 business days past estimated date are prioritized for carrier investigation.

## 4. Escalation Conditions
- Lost packages, un-updated tracking for 7+ days, incorrect delivery address updates, or marked delivered without receiving package require support ticket escalation.
