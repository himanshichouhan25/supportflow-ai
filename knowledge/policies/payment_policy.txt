# PAYMENT POLICY

## 1. Supported Payment Methods & Statuses
- Supported methods include UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, and Wallet.
- Status SUCCESS: Transaction confirmed by bank gateway.
- Status PENDING: Transaction awaiting bank settlement.
- Status FAILED: Transaction declined; no money debited from account.
- Status REFUNDED: Money returned to original payment method.

## 2. Payment Mismatch & Debited Failed Payments
- If payment status is FAILED but money was deducted from customer account, auto-reversal occurs within 3-5 business days.
- If refund is approved for a SUCCESS payment, refund is issued to original payment source.

## 3. Escalation Conditions
- Double charging, payment settlement delays exceeding 5 days, or payment status mismatches require support ticket escalation.
