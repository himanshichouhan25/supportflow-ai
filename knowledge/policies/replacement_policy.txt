# REPLACEMENT POLICY

## 1. Replacement Eligibility Rules
- Product replacements are available strictly for orders with status DELIVERED.
- Orders in PENDING, CONFIRMED, or CANCELLED status are not eligible for replacement.
- The item must have a verified defect, damage during transit, or incorrect item delivery.

## 2. Duplicate Replacement Protection
- Only one active replacement request (REP number) is allowed per order.
- Orders with an active replacement status (REQUESTED, APPROVED, PROCESSING, SHIPPED, DELIVERED) cannot submit a duplicate replacement request.
- CANCELLED replacement requests may be resubmitted if valid.

## 3. Product & Account Validation
- The requested replacement product_id must match the original product ordered.
- Ownership verification ensures the requesting customer_id matches the order record.

## 4. Replacement Request Status
- Created replacement requests are initialized with status REQUESTED and a unique REP identifier.
- Once approved, replacement items are processed and dispatched with tracking details.

## 5. Escalation Conditions
- Out-of-stock replacement items, disputed defect claims, or requests for non-delivered orders trigger support ticket escalation.
